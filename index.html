Who we help

Built for Northern Ireland workshops and yards that need selective metal cleaning without blasting the whole site.

Automotive

Chassis, undersides & restorers

Selective rust and coating removal on classic / kit / 4×4 sections. Quote after photos — we won’t pretend a 300W suitcase strips a whole car in a day.

 Engineering

Fab shops & plant

Weld prep, post-weld discoloration, oil film on jigs and frames. Best recurring work: half-day or day retainers at your premises.

 Agricultural

Gates, trailers & implements

Farm gates, trailer chassis and implements cleaned in situ where access allows. Messy blast alternatives stay in the yard.

 Heritage

Selective specialty work

Decorative iron and agreed specialty cleans after a coupon test. Not our launch niche — metal jobs first.

How a quote works

1. Photos

Send clear shots (and a short video if useful) of the area to clean, including access and power if known.

2. Straight reply

We reply with a guide price band or a decline if the job is outside 300W pulsed handheld capability.

3. On-site confirm

We set exclusion, PPE and extraction, confirm scope, then clean. No grit left in threads or electrics.
