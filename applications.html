Application 01

Weld cleaning — pre-weld & post-weld discoloration

Clear light oxide, HAZ discoloration and thin film so joints look right and coatings stick. Ideal for fab shops that want clean beads without grit left in threads or nearby electrics.

  • Precision on the weld line and HAZ — not a whole-shop blast
  • Less grit mess in fixtures, floors and adjacent components
  • Selective: clean the joint, leave sound coating elsewhere when agreed
  • Photo quote first; small free test patch for qualified trade
  • Fits half-day or short mobile visits at your premises

When 300W is not right: heavy slag that needs mechanical prep first, deep pits, or continuous production-line throughput.

Brand panel · not a job photo A Automotive

Application 02

Automotive & restoration parts

Selective rust and thin coating removal on chassis sections, brackets, undersides and classic / kit / 4×4 components — where access and coating thickness suit a handheld 300W pulsed laser.

  • Precision around mounts, seams and repair areas
  • Less abrasive dust than blasting in a shared workshop
  • Selective: work the problem areas, not the whole car in a day
  • Honest photo quotes — we won’t claim a suitcase strips a full body shell overnight
  • Test patch available so you can judge the finish before booking more hours

When 300W is not right: full-vehicle strip programmes, thick underseal layers, or lead-based coatings as a marketed service.

Application 03

Engineering / fab / plant components

Frames, jigs, brackets and plant parts with light–moderate rust, oil film or thin coatings before repair, inspection or re-coat. Recurring half-days are often the best commercial fit.

  • Precision on edges, holes and mating faces
  • Less grit embed than abrasive methods in a fab shop
  • Selective cleaning on the areas that matter for the next process
  • Photo quote + optional free test patch for qualified commercial work
  • Mobile to your yard — one induction, then efficient beam-on time

When 300W is not right: production mould lines, thick mill scale at production speed, or large continuous coating programmes.

Application 04

Agricultural gates, trailers & implements

Farm gates, trailer chassis and implements cleaned in situ where power and access allow — practical prep before paint or repair without turning the yard into a blast bay.

  • Precision on hinges, rails and repair patches
  • Less grit left in the yard and livestock areas
  • Selective: focus on rusted or coated sections that need work
  • Photo quotes from clear shots of the gate / trailer / implement
  • Mobile call-out from about £175 or half day from about £400 (guides — confirm after photos)

When 300W is not right: whole-fleet strip days with heavy scale, or coatings that need specialised waste handling we have not scoped.
